# Manage ACMAD Institutional Content

This guide covers leadership, Board, staff presentation, organization pages, institutional history, supporting documents,
service links, GIS applications, and CUIP-related content.

## Ownership and review

Assign a communications owner and an institutional approver. Leadership titles, biographies, portraits, Board roles,
official history, partner identities, and programme claims must be approved before publication.

## Leadership and Director General content

Use the dedicated leadership/profile page where available. Maintain:

- official name and title;
- approved portrait and crop;
- biography and message;
- appointment/term information where required;
- professional links and contact policy;
- SEO title, description, and social image.

Preview portrait crops at card, detail, and mobile sizes. Do not replace the original image merely to correct one crop;
use supported focal-point/crop controls when possible.

Homepage Director General content and the organization profile may be separate fields. After changing or offboarding a
person, review both locations. Staff offboarding does not rewrite independently authored homepage text.

## Board of Governors

Maintain the Board landing page and president/leadership details using the dedicated page types in the accepted release.
Verify role names, regional/institutional representation, biography flow, portrait rights, navigation, and publication
approval. Avoid duplicating a Board member as unrelated free text when a structured record exists.

## Staff presentation

The **Our Team** page selects and orders existing staff records. Profile details, account linking, invitations, review,
and offboarding are managed through **Staff Profiles**. See the Staff Profile and Staff Workspace guides.

Removing a person from the page selection hides the public card but does not offboard the person or delete retained
history. Use the correct staff workflow for employment status changes.

## Organization pages

For About, partners, projects, vacancies, tenders, directories, and related pages:

1. Use the intended structured page type.
2. Keep headings and summaries concise and avoid embedding layout HTML in rich text.
3. Use canonical internal page choosers instead of copying internal URLs.
4. Record external owner and review date for external links.
5. Preview navigation, cards, search results, and mobile presentation.

## Institutional timeline and supporting documents

Timeline entries may support multiple documents in the candidate release. For each milestone:

- use the authoritative year/date and approved description;
- attach only relevant, publishable documents;
- give every document a descriptive title and correct language;
- avoid duplicate uploads of the same file;
- verify file accessibility and permissions;
- retain provenance and approval evidence.

When migrating from a single-document schema, confirm all prior documents remain attached after migrations and that
older revisions do not reintroduce obsolete data.

## Products, services, GIS links, and CUIP

Product/service pages can expose editor-managed GIS applications and related institutional content. For each link:

- confirm the destination is publicly authorized and uses HTTPS;
- record the responsible system owner;
- provide an understandable label and summary;
- test authentication expectations and mobile behavior;
- define what should display during an outage.

CUIP content must use approved rationale, activities, joining actions, and galleries. Confirm whether a link is an
informational resource, registration action, or external system before choosing its presentation.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Institutional owner approved names, roles, dates, and claims.
- [ ] Portrait/image rights and accessible descriptions are recorded.
- [ ] Internal links use canonical page selections.
- [ ] External links have owners and review dates.
- [ ] Timeline documents are complete, deduplicated, and correctly titled.
- [ ] Staff membership and employment status use the correct workflows.
- [ ] GIS/CUIP destinations, outage behavior, and access expectations are documented.
- [ ] Desktop, mobile, search, navigation, and social-preview presentation are verified.
